# EcoCrew judge demo plan

## The one-line story

EcoCrew turns one small recycling action into a repeatable habit by checking bottle preparation, rewarding the completed action, and making progress visible to a crew.

## Before judges arrive

1. Open the deployed app on https://lifehack2026.onrender.com.
2. Confirm the app is connected to the prepared demo environment and that the **Clean Bottle Check** appears.
3. Start from a fresh demo account/session. If using local mock mode, reset from the sign-in page with **Reset local demo data**, then refresh.
4. Have one empty bottle and one bottle with water ready. If needed, use the scan page’s **Use a demo example** controls instead.

## What to show each judge (about 2–3 minutes)

### 1. Set the context — 15 seconds

Say:

> “EcoCrew helps people practise the bottle-recycling habit with friends. Today’s action is to empty a single-use plastic bottle, recycle it, and take a photo to confirm the action.”

If the account is new, create an account and enter a display name. Join the seeded crew with invite code `ECO123` if the crew is not already attached.

### 2. Show that preparation matters — 30 seconds

From Home, select **Start today’s action**. On the Scan screen, choose **Bottle with water** under **Use a demo example**, or upload the prepared water-bottle photo. Select **Check my action**.

Point out the result:

- **Empty the bottle first.**
- The bottle is recognised, but visible liquid fails the mission.
- No points are awarded, and the user can retry.

Use this line: “The app is checking the behaviour we care about—not just whether it sees a bottle.”

### 3. Complete the action — 45 seconds

Retry with an empty bottle, or choose **Empty bottle** from the demo examples. Select **Check my action**. On the result screen, explain that the photo validates preparation and context, then click **I recycled it**.

Call out the visible payoff:

- Recycling guidance and a preparation tip.
- Points for the action, preparation, and first action of the day (normally **25 points** on a fresh demo).
- Personal streak and crew progress update.
- The **Leaf Frame** reward appears as the cosmetic unlock.

### 4. Show the social loop — 30 seconds

Open **Crew** and show the seeded teammates, shared streak, weekly mission, and activity feed. Tap one **👏** reaction. Open **Invite** briefly to show that the crew can be shared by link/code.

Open **League** if time allows and point out the weekly standings and the Monday reset. The goal is to make the next action feel social and repeatable.

### 5. Show measurement honestly — 20 seconds

Open **Measurement** and show the seeded baseline/follow-up comparison. Say:

> “This is demonstration data for the measurement design, not observed impact. We track correct preparation and self-reported recycling, with a provisional target of a 20-point improvement.”

## Optional third state

To demonstrate the unrelated-item path, choose **Unrelated item** before completing the daily action. The result should say **That item does not match today’s action.** and offer a retry with zero points. Do not try to run all three fixtures after a successful check-in without resetting the demo or using a separate fresh account; one daily action is intentionally enforced.

## What judges should remember

- The target behaviour is emptying and recycling a single-use plastic bottle.
- The photo checks preparation and recycling context; the final disposal check-in is self-reported.
- Points and streaks make the habit engaging, while the crew creates accountability.
- Images are not presented as proof of real-world disposal and are not stored as part of the profile history.
- If a live AI or upload call fails, use the retry path; do not claim a failure is a validation result.

## Team handoff

- One person drives the app.
- One person narrates the behaviour-change story and points out the judge-facing outcomes.
- One person watches the timer and keeps the demo on the golden path.

End on the crew screen or reward moment, then invite questions.
